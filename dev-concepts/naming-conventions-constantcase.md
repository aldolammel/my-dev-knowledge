#### Dev concepts > Naming conventions
# `CONSTANT_CASE` (or `SCREAMING_SNAKE_CASE`)

---

**Format:**
- All uppercase.
- Words separated by underscores.

**Commonly used in:**
- Constants. 
- Environment variables.
- Configuration flags.
