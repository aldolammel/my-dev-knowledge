It's organized by "category" followed by its data types available.

**Integer:**
- `tinyint`
- `smallint`
- `int`
- `bigint`
- `varint`

**Exact decimal:**
- `decimal`

**Floating point:**
- `float`
- `double`

**Monetary:**
- ??????????????????????????

**Boolean:**
- `boolean`

**Fixed-length text:**
- ==Not supported!==

**Variable text:**
- `text`
- `varchar`
- `ascii`

**Binary:**
- `blob`

**Date:**
- `date`

**Time:**
- `time`
- `duration`
- `timeuuid`

**Date + time:**
- `timestamp`

**UUID:**
- `uuid`

**JSON:**
- `text`

**Array:**

**Map/dictionary:**
